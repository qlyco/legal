# PRIVACY POLICY

## Data Collected

QLYCO Connect collects the following data from our users:
- Email Address
- Display Name
- Passwords

## Data Usage

QLYCO Connect uses the collected data for the following:
- Account Creation & Management: Users create their account using their email address and receives account-related emails (account recovery, verification, etc) when needed.
- Fraud & Abuse Prevention: Access to some parts of our services are limited to registered users to prevent fraudulent uses.

## Data Disclosure

QLYCO Connect does not share users data with third party services, affiliates, or business partners.
